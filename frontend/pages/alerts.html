Latest Scam Alerts

Stay informed about the newest threats

📧 Fake Bank Verification Email

Phishing emails claiming urgent account verification

Red Flags: Suspicious links, urgent tone, requests for credentials

📱 Fake Delivery SMS

Scammers impersonating delivery services

Red Flags: Shortened URLs, unexpected messages, urgency

📱 Instagram Account Scam

Fake messages threatening account deletion

Red Flags: Account deletion threats, verification links, time pressure

🌐 Fake Website

Fraudulent websites impersonating legitimate companies

Red Flags: URL typos, poor design, payment requests
