About This Project

What is Social Engineering Simulator?

Social Engineering Simulator is an educational platform designed to help individuals recognize and defend against social engineering attacks through realistic simulations and interactive learning. It provides a safe environment to practice identifying phishing emails, SMS scams, fake websites, and other common attack vectors.

🎯 Interactive Simulations

Face realistic social engineering scenarios in a safe, controlled environment. Each scenario is designed to test your awareness and decision-making skills.

📊 Detailed Feedback

After each scenario, receive comprehensive explanations of the attack, why your choice was correct or incorrect, and practical tips for protection.

📈 Progress Tracking

Monitor your cybersecurity awareness level with detailed scoring and an awareness assessment that helps you identify areas for improvement.

🛡️ Real-World Threats

Learn about current threat landscape including phishing, SMS scams, fake websites, baiting attacks, and pretexting techniques used by attackers.
